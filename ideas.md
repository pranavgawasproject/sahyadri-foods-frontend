# Sahyadri Foods — Design Direction

## Ground-truth reference specification

This storefront follows the supplied Sahyadri Foods desktop and mobile reference images. It is a premium Indian dry-fruits and healthy-snacks brand with an editorial, farm-to-pouch character. The website must preserve the visual cues that make the reference distinctive: deep forest-green framing, warm cream paper backgrounds, mountain-and-farm visual storytelling, product-first composition, rounded-but-reserved cards, and dense but approachable commerce information.

## Chosen Approach: The Farm-to-Pouch Almanac

### Design Movement

Modern Indian botanical packaging meets an editorial farm journal. The interface combines the reference’s retail clarity with an organic, collected quality rather than using a generic ecommerce template.

### Core Principles

1. **Nature carries the commerce story.** Mountains, fields, botanical silhouettes, and warm ingredient photography establish provenance before price.
2. **Product packages are the protagonists.** Each card is staged like a small market shelf, with legible hierarchy around product name, price, and action.
3. **Trust appears at a human scale.** Quality, hygiene, delivery, and sourcing markers use restrained illustrated iconography and concise reassurance.
4. **Density is balanced by rhythm.** A long home page is divided into distinct chapters with breathing room, alternating pale parchment and saturated green fields.

### Color Philosophy

Forest green communicates provenance, quality, and confidence, while ivory, oat, and soft beige keep the storefront edible and tactile. Muted gold is reserved for small moments of warmth such as ratings and value cues. Green is never used merely as decoration; it frames purchasing decisions and brand commitments.

### Layout Paradigm

The page reads as a vertical market journey rather than a centered stack of generic sections: a panoramic origin story opens the experience, an overlapping proof strip bridges into a collection shelf, then the content alternates between product merchandising, purchasing rationale, and farm provenance. Desktop uses offset editorial columns; mobile resolves into a single, clearly sequenced purchasing path.

### Signature Elements

* A layered mountain-and-farm hero with a pale ingredient tray anchored in the foreground.
* Small herb-sprig dividers and circular ingredient windows that echo food labels rather than dashboard widgets.
* Pressed-paper product cards with green shop actions and slim story labels.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel like handling a curated grocery shelf: hover lifts remain subtle, buttons have a short tactile press, and product cards reveal a little more intent without making the page feel like an app dashboard. All non-functional commerce controls should provide an honest “frontend preview” acknowledgment.

### Animation

On initial load, hero copy, the product tray, and trust strip may enter through soft vertical fades with 40–70 ms stagger. Card hover states can rise by 2–4 px and gently deepen their shadow. Motion is capped below 240 ms and disabled for reduced-motion preferences; no looping visual effects are used.

### Typography System

**DM Serif Display** provides the handcrafted editorial tone for major headlines and select product category labels. **Manrope** supports dense retail information, buttons, and utility navigation with clean, high-legibility letterforms. Headlines use a confident mixed-case rhythm; purchase information remains compact and calm.

### Brand Essence

**Sahyadri Foods brings premium, transparently sourced dry fruits from western India’s farm country to everyday rituals.** Personality: grounded, nourishing, assured.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are sensory and origin-led; CTAs are direct but unhurried. Avoid hollow promotional language.

> "The goodness of the hills, packed for your table."

> "Choose a pouch. Taste the provenance."

### Wordmark & Logo

The brand mark is a dark-green line illustration of three Sahyadri peaks rising above a single leaf and sun disc. The wordmark is a custom-feeling serif-and-small-cap composition paired with a small “Premium Dry Fruits & Healthy Snacks” descriptor.

### Signature Brand Color

**Sahyadri Canopy — #0D4A2F**

## Style Decisions

* Product pouches will consistently carry a Sahyadri mountain-and-leaf stamp, product name, botanical edge treatment, premium grade, and pouch weight so packaging feels shelf-ready rather than generic.
* Trust, comparison, and process content will use pressed-paper surfaces, compact botanical marks, and chapter-note rhythms instead of dashboard-like feature tiles.
* Copy will privilege sensory provenance and everyday rituals. Broad generic claims are replaced with descriptions of sorting, hill-grown ingredients, considered packing, and the journey to the table.
